HOW IT WORKS

Each 5-day Cardano epoch, veDROME holders vote DROME emissions toward gauges. LPs in winning gauges earn emissions; voters collect the pool's swap fees plus any bribes. Lock longer for more power — mercenary capital gets nothing.

TOKENOMICS

ve(3,3) on Cardano

DROME is emitted each 5-day epoch to gauges by veDROME vote. Lockers get a rebase offsetting dilution, 100% of trading fees, and all bribes for gauges they vote on. No team farm — emissions only flow where voters point them.

POOLS

Stable + volatile

Stable pools (x³y + xy³ ≈ k) for pegged assets like DJED/iUSD with 0.02–0.05% fees; volatile pools (x·y = k) for everything else at 0.30%. Concentrated-liquidity tick ranges are live in the Liquidity tab (v2).

GOVERNANCE

5-day epochs

Cardano-native 5-day epochs. Vote, claim, and re-lock on epoch boundaries. veNFTs are transferable — vote power trades on secondary markets.
